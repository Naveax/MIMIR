A. CURRENT TRUTH

- Current scoreboard component ordering is deterministic today.
- The current source of that determinism is implementation-backed:
  - persisted `score_signals` are carried in `BTreeMap<String, f64>`
  - `WeightedSumScorer::score(...)` iterates those `BTreeMap` signals and writes `ScoreVector.components` into another `BTreeMap<String, f64>`
  - persisted scoreboard component maps therefore serialize in lexical key order under the current implementation
- Repo-local evidence supporting that statement:
  - `executor_stage93_scoreboard_component_schema.txt` explicitly documents the three `BTreeMap<String, f64>` points and states that serialized key iteration is lexicographically ordered rather than insertion-ordered
  - `crates/mimir-score/src/lib.rs::WeightedSumScorer::score(...)` builds `ScoreVector.components` with `BTreeMap`
  - current Stage77 score tests in `crates/mimir-cli/src/vertical_slice.rs` assert component-key vectors as `["coverage", "stability"]` on both rows before and after targeted score mutations
  - `executor_deterministic_family_closure_summary.txt`, `executor_deterministic_family_contract_index.txt`, and `executor_deterministic_family_matrix_freeze.txt` all classify scoreboard component ordering as deterministic but only partial as an explicit contract surface
