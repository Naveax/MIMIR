exact decision classes chosen
- `malformed`
- `reject`
- `abstain`
- `accept_candidate`

exact confidence representation chosen
- `LowBoostRecoveryConfidenceBandV1`
- bands:
  - `insufficient_evidence`
  - `boundary_sensitive`
  - `boundary_stable`
- the confidence band is intentionally validator-bounded only
- it represents structural consistency plus boundary stability only
- it does not represent physics truth, replay truth, contact truth, low-boost truth, or recovery
  success truth

exact abstain triggers chosen
- abstain occurs only after the parameter bundle and variant set both survive malformed checks
- abstain occurs when the exact observed-window variant clears the reject floor but the required
  one-frame trim probes are too boundary-sensitive to support provisional acceptance
- concrete v1 trigger:
  - exact window duration is 2 frames
  - both trim probes remain structurally valid
  - at least one required trimmed window duration is below the validator stability floor of 2
    frames

what was intentionally excluded
- any rollout-backed or RocketSim-backed feasibility proof
- any claim of true low-boost confirmation
- any claim of contact truth or recovery success truth
- any generic validator or generic confidence framework
- any new replay parsing, replay mining, export, curriculum, runtime, or CLI work
- any speculative metadata bags or opaque score payloads

whether Rust type shaping and narrow implementation were added, and why
- Yes.
- Added `LowBoostRecoveryValidationDecisionV1`,
  `LowBoostRecoveryConfidenceBandV1`,
  `LowBoostRecoveryValidationReasonV1`,
  `LowBoostRecoveryValidationNoteV1`, and
  `LowBoostRecoveryValidationResultV1` in `crates/mimir-types/src/lib.rs`.

  `validate_low_boost_recovery_candidate_v1`.
- The implementation stays family-specific so this pass does not quietly create fake all-family
  infrastructure.
